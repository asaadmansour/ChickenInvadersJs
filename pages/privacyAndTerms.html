PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: January 25, 2026

Information We Collect
This game collects and stores minimal data to enhance your gaming experience:

  • LOCAL

    Settings preferences (music/sound effects mute status) stored in browser's Local Storage

  • SCORES

    Player name and score submitted to Firebase Firestore for leaderboard display

  • NO PII

    We do NOT collect email addresses, IP addresses, or any personal identification information

How We Use Your Data
• Your settings are stored locally to remember your audio preferences across sessions
• Your submitted scores are stored in our Firebase database to display on the public leaderboard
• Player names on the leaderboard are publicly visible to all users
• No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising purposes

Third-Party Services
This game uses Google Firebase (Firestore) to store leaderboard scores. Firebase may collect analytics data according to their privacy policy. External links to Discord, GitHub, and YouTube are subject to their respective privacy policies.

Data Retention
Leaderboard scores are retained indefinitely. Local storage data persists until you clear your browser cache or storage.

Your Rights
You can clear local storage data at any time through your browser settings. For leaderboard score removal requests, contact us via Discord.
